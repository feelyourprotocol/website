# Two Audiences

Feel Your Protocol is built for **humans and agents equally**. The [two legs](/vision/two-legs) are the surfaces (textbook and lab). This page is the other axis: **who the customer is**.

Agents are first-class customers. They discover services, pay, evaluate results, and recommend tools to other agents. Humans — researchers, educators, integrators, the token community — are the other customer. Neither path is a proxy for the other.

## Equal customers

Every surface has a human close **and** an agent close. Neither requires the other.

| Need | Human | Agent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find | Website, X, DevRel | Registry, probe, other agents |
| Trust | Brand, explorations, founder | Provenance, spec pins, repeatable results |
| Pay | Optional token discount lane, once a paid tier exists | At launch: nothing to pay. Later, [x402](/concepts/x402) quote and retry on new EIPs — no dashboard |
| Start | Connect page, host config | Machine-readable capability, price, and payment |

The design test: **can an agent finish this without a human who has read our website?** Launch week may leave some of that incomplete. The default we build toward is yes.

## Where this sits

- **Launch week** is the hosted HTTP door and an honest Glamsterdam catalogue, open to connect. [x402](/concepts/x402) opens with the paid tier a few weeks later. The full agent-to-agent market is the direction after that door is open.
- **Two legs** are the surfaces: website as textbook and DevRel, MCP as lab.
- **Token** is a [discount](/monetization/token), never a gate.
- **Discovery, payment quotes, and onboarding** are product. See [Principles](/vision/principles) (*Agents are customers*) and the [roadmap board](/roadmap/roadmap).
